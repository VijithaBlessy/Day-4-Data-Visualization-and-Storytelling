# Day 4 – Data Visualization and Storytelling

## Project Title
**Superstore Sales – Business Performance Story**

## Objective
To create meaningful visualizations that communicate sales trends, regional performance, product category performance, and business insights using Power BI.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Sample Superstore Dataset

## Project Overview
This project analyses the Sample Superstore dataset and presents business performance through a five-page visual storytelling report.

## Report Pages
1. **Business Overview** – Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity and overall performance.
2. **Regional Analysis** – Sales and profit comparison across regions.
3. **Category Analysis** – Sales and profit comparison across product categories.
4. **Monthly Sales Trend** – Analysis of sales variations over time.
5. **Business Insights and Recommendations** – Key findings and recommendations.

## Key Insights
- The West region generates the highest sales and profit.
- The South region records the lowest sales.
- Technology is the highest-performing category in sales and profit.
- Monthly sales fluctuate over time.

## Deliverables
- `Superstore_Sales_Performance_Dashboard.pbix`
- `Superstore_Data_Storytelling_Report.pdf`

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how data visualization and storytelling can transform business data into meaningful insights and support better decision-making.
